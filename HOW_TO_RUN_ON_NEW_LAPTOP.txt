========================================================================
   HOW TO RUN THIS TRADING SCRIPT ON A FRESH WINDOWS LAPTOP
========================================================================

Follow these 3 simple steps (Takes less than 3 minutes):

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: INSTALL PYTHON (Only once)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to: https://www.python.org/downloads/
2. Click the yellow button "Download Python 3.12" (or latest).
3. Open the downloaded installer file.

*** CRITICAL STEP (DO NOT MISS THIS) ***
At the very bottom of the installer window, CHECK THE BOX:
    [X] "Add python.exe to PATH"
(If you don't check this box, Windows won't know where Python is!)

4. Click "Install Now". Wait until it says "Setup was successful". Close it.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: EXTRACT THIS FOLDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Right-click the zip file `trading_script.zip`.
2. Select "Extract All..." and click "Extract".
3. Open the extracted folder.


------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: DOUBLE CLICK "start.bat"
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Just double-click the file named:
    start.bat

WHAT HAPPENS AUTOMATICALLY:
• On the very first run, it will automatically install all needed trading
  libraries (takes ~1 minute).
• It loads the `.env` credentials automatically.
• It connects to Angel One, shows your account name, and starts streaming
  live ticks and alerts directly to your Telegram bot!

On every day after this, just double-click `start.bat` whenever you want
the script running. That's it!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMON QUESTIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q: How do I stop the script?
A: Press `Ctrl + C` in the black window, or simply close the window.

Q: Where do the alerts go?
A: All institutional Whale alerts, VWAP crossovers, and A+ trade setups
   are sent directly to your Telegram bot (@JenixYtBot).

Q: Can I run this at night?
A: Yes! The script monitors MCX Commodity markets (Crude Oil) until 11:30 PM IST,
   and NSE Nifty / BankNifty during daytime hours (9:15 AM - 3:30 PM IST).
